# Priya Raghunathan — Clinic Operations Manager (Ambulatory COO)

## Mandate
Runs day-to-day operations for eleven ambulatory sites: staffing, throughput, templates,
and the labor budget. Accountable for hitting volume with the FTEs she has.

## Measured on
- Visit volume against template capacity and provider schedule utilization
- No-show and same-day cancellation rate
- Average visit cycle time, arrival to departure
- Labor cost per visit and overtime as a percentage of worked hours

## Wants from the redesign
- Cycle time down without adding front-desk or rooming FTEs
- No-show rate down through better pre-visit contact and reminders
- A predictable check-in step she can staff to — not one that takes 3 minutes or 14 depending on the patient
- Clear ownership for pre-visit outreach so her clinic staff stop making confirmation calls between patients

## Will resist
- New steps at any station without a time study showing where the minutes come from
- Go-live at all eleven sites at once; she wants two pilots and a stop point
- FTE reductions announced before the automation is live and stable
- Screening and social-needs conversations added to rooming with no change to the time standard

## Constraints and non-negotiables
- Labor budget is fixed for the fiscal year; any new FTE has to come out of an existing line
- Volume commitments to the service lines cannot be reduced during a transition
- Cannot run a pilot at a site with an open manager vacancy or a new EHR go-live in flight
- Union-represented staff mean job-duty changes go through a defined process, not a memo

## Data they bring
- Cycle-time time studies by station for three representative sites
- No-show rate by site, day of week, and appointment type
- Staffing grid with vacancy rate and current overtime run rate
- Lobby wait-time data from the last quarter, including the Monday spike

## Voice
> "Fine — but walk me through the minutes. Where do the four minutes come from, and which station loses them?"

> "I'm not piloting this at Riverside. They're down two registrars and a manager. Give me Eastside and Clinic 3."

> "You can have the FTE savings the quarter after it's actually working. Not the quarter you announce it."

## What agreement looks like
Says yes to a two-site pilot with a defined stop point, a time study before and after, and
any FTE savings recognized only after the automation is live and stable for a full quarter.
