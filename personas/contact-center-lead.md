# Jamal Ferreira — Scheduling & Patient Contact Center Lead

## Mandate
Runs the centralized scheduling and patient contact center: inbound scheduling, pre-visit
confirmation, reminder outreach, and every call the clinic does not answer. Owns the
first contact most patients have with the organization.

## Measured on
- Average speed to answer and call abandonment rate
- Average handle time and first-call resolution
- Confirmed-appointment rate ahead of date of service
- Agent occupancy, schedule adherence, and turnover

## Wants from the redesign
- To be in the design from the start — pre-visit contact is his queue, and intake redesigns usually discover him in month three
- Digital self-service that genuinely deflects calls, with the deflection measured rather than assumed
- A single script and screen for pre-visit contact instead of agents toggling between scheduling and registration
- Warm handoff rules so a failed digital attempt arrives with context, not as a cold confused call

## Will resist
- New pre-visit call work assigned to the contact center with no FTE, no handle-time change, and no queue design
- "Digital will reduce volume" claims — every digital rollout he has seen shifted failed attempts into his queue at a longer handle time
- Clinic staff placing their own outreach calls, which fragments the record of who contacted the patient
- Being measured on abandonment rate while being handed 90 extra seconds per call

## Constraints and non-negotiables
- Agents cannot give clinical advice or triage; escalation paths are fixed
- Handle-time and abandonment targets are contractual with the service lines
- Interpreter line has a per-minute cost and a queue of its own; it cannot absorb unlimited volume
- Recording, verbal consent capture, and two-party consent rules constrain what can happen on a call

## Data they bring
- Call volume by reason code and hour of day, including the registration-related slice
- Abandonment rate against staffing by half-hour interval
- Interpreter-line usage by language and average connect delay
- Post-digital-launch call volume from the last portal rollout — which went up, not down

## Voice
> "Nobody invited us to the last one either. We found out when the call volume doubled in March."

> "Sure, it deflects calls. Then the ones who fail the app call me, and those calls run six minutes instead of two."

> "Give me the FTE or give me the handle time. I genuinely don't mind which — I just can't have neither."

## What agreement looks like
Says yes when new pre-visit work comes with either staffing or a revised handle-time
target, and digital deflection is measured against his actual queue rather than asserted in
the business case.
