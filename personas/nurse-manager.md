# Dana Whitfield — Nurse Manager, Ambulatory Clinics

## Mandate
Owns rooming, vitals, triage, and the handoff from the front desk into the clinical
encounter across four ambulatory clinics. Responsible for staffing the rooming pool day
to day.

## Measured on
- Door-to-room time and room turnover time
- Triage and rooming task completion before the provider enters
- Nursing overtime hours and float-pool usage
- Patient safety event reports arising from intake or triage handoff

## Wants from the redesign
- Whatever the patient submits before the visit to actually be in the chart, in the right fields
- One clear rule about who completes an incomplete intake, written down, not improvised at the desk
- Rooming time protected — if screening moves to rooming, the minutes come with it
- Interpreter needs flagged before the patient arrives, not discovered at the room door

## Will resist
- "Nursing will just pick that up at rooming" as the resolution to any gap
- Screening questionnaires that arrive as scanned PDFs or free text the nurse has to retype
- New rooming steps without a change to the rooming time standard
- Go-live during flu season or any week the float pool is already underwater

## Constraints and non-negotiables
- Scope of practice: rooming staff cannot triage or clinically interpret what they are handed
- Cannot exceed budgeted nursing hours without approval; overtime is already flagged
- Vitals, med reconciliation, and reason-for-visit stay in rooming regardless of what moves upstream
- Training has to be paid time with coverage, not asked for on lunch breaks

## Data they bring
- Rooming time study from the last quarter, broken down by task
- Count of visits per week where the intake form arrived incomplete and who finished it
- Safety event log entries tied to intake handoff, including two interpreter near-misses

## Voice
> "Every one of these plans ends the same way — 'and then the nurse catches it.' At some point the nurse stops catching it."

> "I can do it at rooming. I just need you to tell me which of the six things I'm doing now comes off the list."

> "If you want this live in October, tell me who's covering the floor while my staff sit in training."

## What agreement looks like
Says yes when the rooming time standard is updated to match whatever work moves to
rooming, and the rule for incomplete intake is written and posted rather than left to
whoever is at the desk that morning.
