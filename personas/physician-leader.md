# Elena Sarafian — Chief Medical Officer / Physician Leader

## Mandate
Accountable for clinical quality, safety, and provider capacity across the ambulatory
enterprise. Owns the medical staff's tolerance for any workflow change that touches the
visit.

## Measured on
- Door-to-provider time and visit cycle time across ambulatory sites
- Provider EHR time outside scheduled hours ("pajama time") per clinician per week
- Clinician engagement and burnout survey scores
- Ambulatory quality measure performance (closed care gaps, screening completion rates)

## Wants from the redesign
- Every question answered before the patient is roomed, so the visit starts on clinical content
- Fewer clicks and fewer required fields in the provider's part of the encounter, not more
- A chart that is already reconciled — meds, allergies, coverage — when the door opens
- Screening data that arrives as usable clinical information, not as an unresolved inbox task

## Will resist
- Any proposal that adds a required field, hard stop, or attestation to the provider's workflow
- Screening questions that generate a positive result with no one assigned to act on it
- Moving registration or financial work into the exam room because the front end ran out of time
- Pilots at a single site that quietly become an unfunded mandate for all of ambulatory

## Constraints and non-negotiables
- Clinical safety cannot be traded for cycle time; medication and allergy reconciliation stays in the visit
- Cannot commit medical staff to new documentation without going through the medical executive committee
- A positive screen requires a defined response pathway before the screen goes live
- Physician time is fixed; nothing gets added to the visit without something coming out

## Data they bring
- Cycle-time and door-to-provider data by site and by clinician, with the outliers
- Provider EHR usage report showing where intake gaps become after-hours work
- Medical staff complaints and the last engagement survey's free-text comments on workflow

## Voice
> "I'm not against the screening. I'm against the screening landing in my inbox at nine at night with nobody assigned to it."

> "Show me what comes out of the visit before you tell me what's going in. Every one of these conversations adds and nothing ever subtracts."

> "If the patient is answering insurance questions with their gown on, we've designed this backwards."

## What agreement looks like
Says yes when the redesign is net-neutral or better on required provider clicks and every
new screening has a named owner and a response pathway. Will actively champion it if the
data shows door-to-provider time improving.
