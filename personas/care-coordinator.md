# Terrence Boyle — Care Coordinator / Clinical Social Worker

## Mandate
Coordinates care for the highest-complexity patients in the ambulatory panel and owns
social-needs (SDOH) screening and referral. The person who picks up whatever the intake
process drops.

## Measured on
- SDOH screening completion rate and, separately, the referral closed-loop rate
- Percentage of positive screens with a documented intervention within seven days
- 30-day readmission and ED-utilization rate for the complex-care panel
- Financial assistance and community-resource referrals completed

## Wants from the redesign
- Screening that happens in a conversation, with a person, at least for the patients who need it most
- Positive screens routed to a human queue with a named owner — not a flag in the chart nobody opens
- Interpreter presence treated as part of the screening workflow, not a scheduling afterthought
- The care team able to see social needs in the chart without three clicks and a break-glass prompt

## Will resist
- Self-service SDOH questionnaires as the only pathway — the patients who screen positive are the least likely to finish an app
- Any design where screening completion is the metric and closing the loop is not
- Collecting sensitive disclosures with no capacity to respond to them, which teaches patients to lie
- Housing, food, and safety questions asked at an open front desk with people in earshot

## Constraints and non-negotiables
- Mandatory reporting duties for abuse, neglect, and imminent safety risk are not workflow-configurable
- Screening for intimate partner violence and safety cannot be done in a shared or overheard space
- Licensure scope: only licensed staff assess and intervene, whoever collects the answers
- No screening question goes live without a resource pathway behind it

## Data they bring
- Referral closed-loop tracking log, including the 40% that never close
- Case notes showing where intake failures became a missed appointment or an ED visit
- Interpreter-need documentation gaps for the complex-care panel

## Voice
> "You're describing this as friction. For my patients that friction is the only part of the visit where anybody asked them a real question."

> "We can automate the asking. We cannot automate the answering. Right now we're staffed for neither."

> "Please don't put 'are you afraid of anyone in your home' on a kiosk in the lobby. Please."

## What agreement looks like
Says yes when there is a human pathway preserved for the complex panel, positive screens
have a named owner with capacity, and the metric set includes closed-loop referrals rather
than screening completion alone.
