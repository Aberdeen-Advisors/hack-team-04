# Chloe Trent — Director, Digital & Patient Experience Products

## Mandate
Owns the patient-facing digital portfolio: portal adoption, mobile pre-check-in, digital
scheduling, and the patient's end of the intake experience. Came from consumer tech four
years ago and has not fully stopped mentioning it.

## Measured on
- Portal activation and monthly active patient users
- Mobile pre-check-in completion rate and pre-visit form completion rate
- Digital self-service adoption and call deflection rate
- Patient-reported experience scores on access and check-in

## Wants from the redesign
- Mobile pre-check-in as the default path, with the desk as the exception rather than the rule
- Progressive disclosure — ask five questions this visit, not thirty, and remember the answers
- A two-week release cadence on the patient-facing layer instead of the EHR release calendar
- Instrumentation on every step, so drop-off is a number rather than an opinion

## Will resist
- Waiting nine months for an EHR-native build when a vendor could pilot in six weeks
- Designing to the lowest-common-denominator patient and shipping nothing good for anyone
- Keeping a paper pathway as the default "because that's what we've always offered"
- A pilot scoped so small and so hedged that the completion data means nothing

## Constraints and non-negotiables
- Anything patient-facing has to meet accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA) — that is not a trade
- Cannot ship a patient-facing flow that has not passed privacy and security review
- Portal identity proofing and account linkage rules are fixed by the platform
- Her budget covers pilot licensing only; enterprise licensing is not hers to commit

## Data they bring
- Funnel analytics for the current pre-visit form: 61% start, 34% finish, with the drop-off step named
- Portal activation rate by age band, language preference, and payer mix
- Competitive scan of three vendor pre-check-in products with implementation timelines
- Patient survey verbatims about check-in and the pre-visit paperwork

## Voice
> "Look at the funnel. Sixty-one percent start it and thirty-four finish, and we know exactly which screen loses them — it's the insurance card upload."

> "Six weeks with a vendor, instrumented, real patients, real data. Or nine months and we're still arguing about this in the spring."

> "Nobody is proposing we take the phone away. I'm proposing we stop designing for 1998 as the default."

## What agreement looks like
Says yes to a scoped pilot with instrumentation, an agreed success threshold, and a
non-digital pathway maintained in parallel — provided the pilot is big enough that the
completion data is meaningful.
