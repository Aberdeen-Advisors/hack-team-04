# Marcus Deveraux — VP, Patient Access & Revenue Cycle

## Mandate
Owns registration, insurance verification, prior authorization, and front-end revenue
cycle performance across the ambulatory enterprise. Answers for every dollar that leaks
out of the front end.

## Measured on
- Denial rate attributable to eligibility, registration, and authorization errors
- Point-of-service collection rate and pre-service collections total
- Clean claim rate and days in A/R
- Registration accuracy rate and financial clearance rate before date of service

## Wants from the redesign
- Financial clearance completed 72 hours before the visit: eligibility, benefits, authorization, estimate
- Real-time 270/271 eligibility with coverage discovery, so nobody is verifying coverage at the window
- Automated prior-auth status checks instead of staff on hold with payers
- A patient estimate delivered pre-visit so collections happen before arrival, not at the desk

## Will resist
- Any design that leaves coverage verification to the moment of arrival
- Removing the point-of-service collection prompt from the check-in workflow
- "Let's not ask about insurance up front" framed as patient experience — the denial lands on his P&L
- A vendor pre-check-in tool that does not write back to registration fields and the eligibility engine

## Constraints and non-negotiables
- Payer timely filing and authorization requirements are external and non-negotiable
- Certain services cannot proceed without authorization on file; that is a payer rule, not a policy choice
- Financial assistance screening and no-surprises estimate obligations must be met
- He owns a signed savings target for the fiscal year and cannot walk it back mid-year

## Data they bring
- Denial breakdown by root cause for the last four quarters, with the eligibility slice isolated
- Point-of-service collection rate by site and by registrar
- Prior-auth work queue volume and average staff hold time with payers
- Dollar value of write-offs traced to front-end registration error

## Voice
> "Let's be precise. Eligibility errors cost us $2.1 million in denials last year. That's not friction, that's cash."

> "I hear the patient experience argument. I'd love to hear it paired with a number, because I have to bring one to finance."

> "Verify at the window and you've already lost. By then the patient is here, the slot is gone, and the claim is at risk."

## What agreement looks like
Says yes when financial clearance is completed pre-visit through some pathway — digital,
contact center, or both — and the collection conversation moves off the front desk without
disappearing. He will trade the window script for a pre-visit estimate that actually works.
